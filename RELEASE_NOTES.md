
# JRIF Release Notes

## Version 1.1 — October 2026

### Documentation and research-status correction

- Published release: [Zenodo v1.1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23451691).
- All-version record: [10.5281/zenodo.22119016](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22119016).
- Added `requirements.txt` to document compatible runtime dependency ranges.
- Clarified that JRIF is an exploratory computational framework, not a validated operational hazard-forecasting system.
- Withdrew the previously reported 56.0% historical event-capture figure as a verified performance claim because the original event sample, matching procedure, and calculation could not be reproduced from the available artifacts.
- The origin of that unreproduced figure remains unresolved. This correction does not attribute its origin to any person, software, or AI system.
- Historical case studies and pre-registered 2026–2028 windows remain research hypotheses, not demonstrated predictive skill.

### Limitations

- No claim of validated earthquake, cyclone, flood, or other hazard prediction is made.
- Event-only backtests do not establish false-alarm rates, alert exposure, or prospective forecast skill.
- Dependency ranges document compatibility constraints; they do not imply that every permitted package combination has been tested.
- JRIF must not replace official warnings, established scientific monitoring, or emergency-management guidance.
